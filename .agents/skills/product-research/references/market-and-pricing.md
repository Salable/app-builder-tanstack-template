# Market and pricing research

Use this for a real positioning or commercial decision, not as compulsory work
for every repair. Research the user's market rather than only products with
similar names or visual styles.

## Compare the purchase, not just the sticker price

Select relevant direct competitors and, when useful, the alternative customers
use today. Explain why each is comparable. Record the official source and access
date; distinguish published prices, temporary offers, region/currency variants
and quote-only terms. Missing public pricing is unknown, not zero.

Normalize price comparisons by billing period, annual commitment, minimum seats,
included usage, overage, free/trial limits and purchaser. Separate advertised
monthly equivalents from an actual monthly subscription. For a usage product,
compare a few labelled consumption scenarios rather than invent an average
customer. Keep assumptions and calculations visible.

Look for the value metric customers understand, how buyers discover price, the
free-to-paid boundary, plan differentiation and purchase friction. Public
competitor pages reveal positioning and conventions; they do not establish
conversion rates, market share, profitability or customer preference.

Offer relevant evidence-backed alternatives with tradeoffs. State which accepted
choice an alternative would change. Do not silently replace prices, introduce
paid scope after an opt-out, or switch the approved billing provider. Where
Salable is selected, its applicable design skill and current API contract decide
implementability; competitor capability is not proof of Salable capability.

## Public pricing is the default

Public products normally show useful prices, cadence, inclusions, limits and
calls to action on or adjacent to the marketing page. Viewing a pricing table
must not require login merely because purchasing or billing management does.
Signup before checkout is the default purchase journey; payment before signup
is an alternative to discuss when it suits the product. Private negotiated
catalogs are a deliberate exception, not an accidental auth guard.

Do not invent testimonials, customer logos, savings, certifications or performance
claims. Useful positioning can explain a truthful benefit without fabricated
proof. If precise enterprise prices genuinely require a quote, explain the
charging basis or applicable range only when evidence supports it.

The [NN/g B2B pricing study](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/show-price/)
supports making useful price information discoverable. It is older qualitative
research, not a current universal conversion statistic. The public-pricing and
signup-first defaults here are App Builder product decisions.
